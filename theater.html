How this works

One click: real YouTube, plus controls that actually stay on top

Browsers require an actual click before a page can open a new window — that's intentional, to stop sites from popping things up on their own. One click here opens YouTube in its own tab and a small control window that uses Picture-in-Picture, so it floats above every other window — including YouTube — automatically, not just the tab it started from.

Your walking controls will float in a small always-on-top window; YouTube opens beside it in a new tab.
