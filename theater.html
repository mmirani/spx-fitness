How this works

Real YouTube, full-size — with your controls floating on top

YouTube won't let any site load its full homepage inside a frame — that's their own security policy. So instead: open YouTube exactly like you normally would, in its own tab, and open a small always-handy control window next to it. Drag the small window on top of the video and walk.

1
Open YouTube
Opens real youtube.com in a new tab — search, browse, and play exactly as usual.
2
Open Floating Controls
A small window with Speed, Start/Stop, and your live stats — drag it on top of the video.
Tip: open both, then drag the small controls window to a corner of the YouTube tab.
